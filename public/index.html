The Mortgage Report

A guided mortgage and savings calculator that ends in a report you can print

This tool turns your mortgage numbers into a printable brief.

Enter your loan to see the full repayment schedule and what it costs you in interest over the years. From there you can test an overpayment, a one-off lump sum, or a change in rate, and project some savings on the side.

The last step gathers it all into a one-page report you can read on screen or save as a PDF.

  1. Mortgage: your loan today
  2. Simulation: try a what-if (optional)
  3. Savings: project a pot (optional)
  4. Report: print or save as PDF
